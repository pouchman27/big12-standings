Where BYU stands
BYU - CFP #10 (Nov 10, 2026 committee rankings) · 7-2 overall, 4-2 in the Big 12
In the 12-team format, seeds 1-4 get a bye, seeds 5-8 host a first-round game on campus, seeds 9-12 go on the road. At #10 BYU is in the field but on the road; the cfb.fyi projected bracket sends them to #7 Miami. Hosting means climbing at least two spots.

Method and sources

Simulation numbers: cfb.fyi playoff projection - the rest of the season played out 10,000 times using each game's betting line and team power ratings, snapshot taken Nov 11, 2026. Seed bands: bye = seeds 1-4, home = 5-8, road = 9-12, out = unseeded. Bid-thief math: ESPN FPI conference-title odds (Nov 9 snapshot) over the Nov 10 CFP rankings. These are model outputs, not committee predictions; the committee decides the real rankings every Tuesday.
