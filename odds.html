BYU playoff odds

The centerpiece: where BYU stands, where their playoff seed lands across 10,000 simulated seasons, the chance of a home game, the chance they fall out, and the chase around them - with the bid-thief watch underneath.

Where BYU stands
BYU - CFP #10 (Nov 10, 2026 committee rankings) · 7-2 overall, 4-2 in the Big 12
In the 12-team format, seeds 1-4 get a bye, seeds 5-8 host a first-round game on campus, seeds 9-12 go on the road. At #10 BYU is in the field but on the road; the cfb.fyi projected bracket sends them to #7 Miami. Hosting means finishing seeded 5-8. Seeds follow the committee ranking, except automatic bids can pull a champion up: their projection seeds Pac-12 champ Boise State #12 despite a #24 ranking.

Method and sources

Simulation numbers: cfb.fyi 2026 playoff projection - their words: "We played out the rest of the season 10,000 times using each game's betting line and team power ratings," updated Nov 11, 2026. Their output is final bracket seeds (with automatic bids applied), not committee-rank movement; no source we found publishes the pairwise odds of one specific team passing another, so those aren't on this page. Seed bands: bye = seeds 1-4, home = 5-8, road = 9-12, out = unseeded. Bid-thief math: ESPN FPI conference-title odds (Nov 9 snapshot) over the Nov 10 CFP rankings. These are model outputs, not committee predictions; the committee decides the real rankings every Tuesday.
