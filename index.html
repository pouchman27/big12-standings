How the Big 12 picks its championship teams

With 16 teams and no round-robin, the Big 12 seeds the two teams with the best conference winning percentage into the championship game. The winner is the conference's automatic qualifier (AQ) to the College Football Playoff. When two or more teams tie for first or second place, the tiebreakers below decide it - in order, stopping as soon as one team has the edge.

Tie for 1stBoth tied teams are in. If they played, the head-to-head winner is the #1 seed. If they didn't play, run the two-team steps below to set the seeding.
No tie for 1st, tie for 2ndThe tied teams run the two-team steps below. The winner is the #2 seed and goes to Arlington.
3+ teams tiedRun the multiple-team steps. The moment one team gains the edge it is seeded, and the rest restart from step A. Down to two teams? Switch to the two-team steps.

Two-team tie

Applied in order until one team wins the tiebreak
A

Head-to-head.Winner of the game between the two tied teams takes the tiebreak.

B

Record vs common conference opponents.Win percentage against every conference opponent both tied teams played.

C

Record vs the next-highest common opponent.Start at the top of the standings and walk down: how did each tied team do against the best-placed common conference opponent? Keep going until it separates them. If the comparison opponent is itself a tied group, use the tied teams' combined record against that whole group, not against the individual teams.

D

Strength of conference schedule.Combined conference win percentage of each tied team's conference opponents.

E

Total wins in the 12-game season.Only one win against an FCS (or lower) team counts per season, and games exempt from the NCAA's maximum-contest rule don't count.

F

SportSource Analytics ranking.Highest team Rating Score after the final regular-season weekend.

G

Coin toss.Yes, really.

Three or more teams tied

As soon as one team is seeded, the rest restart from step A; a tie reduced to two teams switches to the two-team list
A

Record in games among the tied teams.Mini-league win percentage. Two catches: (1) if the tied teams didn't all play each other but one team beat every other tied team, that team is seeded and the rest restart the process; (2) if they didn't all play each other and nobody swept the group, skip to step B.

B

Record vs common conference opponents.Win percentage against conference opponents every tied team played.

C

Record vs the next-highest common opponent.Same walk down the standings as the two-team step C, including the "treat a tied group as one combined opponent" rule.

D

Strength of conference schedule.Combined conference win percentage of each tied team's conference opponents.

E

Total wins in the 12-game season.Same fine print: one FCS win max, exempt games don't count.

F

SportSource Analytics ranking.Highest team Rating Score after the final regular-season weekend.

G

Coin toss.

Good to know

Only conference games count toward the standings that set the tie. Point differential and margin of victory are never used - if records and strength of schedule can't separate teams, it goes to the SportSource Analytics metric, then a literal coin toss.

Source: Big 12 Football Championship Tiebreaker Policies and the conference's official 16-team tiebreaker policy (PDF), in place since the 2024 season.
