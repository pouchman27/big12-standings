CFP remaining schedules

The committee's top 25, in ranking order. Each game's win probability is for the ranked team. The estimate on the right is its chance to win every remaining regular-season game. Tap a "wins" chip to pick a game's winner, or tap the game background to cycle win, loss and unpicked. Games between ranked teams stay linked. The final record on the right adds your picks to the current record; unpicked games show a worst-to-best range.

How the probabilities work

Game win: ESPN Matchup Predictor values, not betting odds. A dash means ESPN has no prediction available.

Win out: our estimate, multiplying the listed game probabilities and assuming independent outcomes. For example, 80% × 70% = 56%. It is not an ESPN season projection or a chance of making the playoff. Injuries, changing team strength and related outcomes can make the true chance different.

Pick winners: tap or click a game to cycle: this team wins, this team loses, then back to no pick. When two ranked teams play each other, the game stays linked on both cards, so picking BYU to beat Utah also counts it as a Utah loss. A picked win counts as 100% and a picked loss as 0% in the win-out estimate; unpicked games keep ESPN's probability. Picks are saved in this browser only; Clear picks resets them.

Regular season only. Conference championships and postseason games are excluded. Completed games are removed on refresh. Dates and any confirmed kickoff times are shown in Eastern time; TBD games retain ESPN's listed game date. No invented 50/50 values are substituted for missing odds.
