Conference opponent win %

This is the Big 12's tiebreak step D: the combined win percentage in conference games of each team's conference opponents - how strong each team's conference schedule has been. Only conference games count, and every conference opponent counts with its full conference record. Tap winners in the remaining games below to change results and watch every team's number move. Results you don't pick simply don't count yet.

Official source: Big 12 Football Championship Tiebreaker Policies (step D) and the 16-team policy PDF. Steps A-C come first - head-to-head (BYU and Houston don't play this season), then records against common opponents, then the walk down the standings - so this lever only decides a tie that survives those steps.
