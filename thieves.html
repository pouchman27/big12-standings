Projected bid thieves

A bid thief (your definition): a team that wins its conference championship game while ranked behind BYU, but still takes a playoff spot. Under the 2026 CFP format the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions are automatic, the highest-ranked champion from the six other leagues is automatic, Notre Dame is in if it finishes in the CFP top 12, and the next seven highest-ranked teams fill the rest. Every champion ranked behind BYU eats one of those spots.

Format: CFP Selection Committee, 2026-27 format and how the field is built. Title odds are ESPN FPI season simulations, not a committee prediction. Rankings are the CFP rankings from Nov 3 (games through Oct 31) - new rankings land Tuesday night, Nov 10, and BYU has since lost at Utah, so expect movement. This is a projection, not a promise.
