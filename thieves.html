Projected bid thieves

A bid thief (your definition): a team that wins its conference championship game while ranked behind BYU, but still takes a playoff spot. Under the 2026 CFP format the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions are automatic, the highest-ranked team from the six other leagues - champion or not - is automatic, Notre Dame is in if it finishes in the CFP top 12, and the highest-ranked teams left fill the rest - seven spots, or six when Notre Dame takes one. Every champion ranked behind BYU eats one of those spots.

Format: CFP Selection Committee, 2026-27 format and how the field is built. Title odds are ESPN FPI season simulations, not a committee prediction. Rankings are the CFP Top 25 from Nov 10, 2026, from the official committee release. This is a projection, not a promise.
