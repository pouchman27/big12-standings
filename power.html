Consensus power ranking

Every FBS team, ranked by combining the raw power ratings of three predictive rating systems: ESPN FPI, Sports Reference’s SRS and Brian Fremeau’s FEI. Each system’s raw numbers are shown with that system’s own rank; the consensus standardizes the three scales and averages them. The Colley Matrix is shown as a fourth column for comparison only: it is built from wins and losses alone, so it behaves like a resume and is not averaged into this power consensus. Tap a column header to sort by that system instead.

Different systems, different scales: FPI is projected point margin vs an average team, SRS is a points-based margin rating, FEI is opponent-adjusted possession efficiency. The consensus z-score puts those three on one scale before averaging. Colley is a 0–1 win-loss-only rating, shown for comparison and excluded from the consensus.

How the consensus works

ESPN FPI (predictive): Football Power Index — expected point margin against an average opponent on a neutral field, built from play-by-play efficiency.

Sports Reference SRS (predictive, margin-based): Simple Rating System — average point differential adjusted for strength of schedule.

FEI (predictive): Fremeau Efficiency Index (BCF Toys) — opponent-adjusted per-possession efficiency. Positive is better; +1.49 led FBS at collection time.

Colley Matrix (record-only, shown for comparison, NOT in the consensus): a bias-free rating built only from wins and losses and who they came against. It uses no margin or stats, so it behaves more like a resume than a prediction — including it would blur what this page measures, so it gets its own column but no weight in the consensus. Its raw rating runs 0–1.

Consensus (ours): each of the three predictive systems’ ratings across all FBS teams is converted to a z-score (how far above or below the FBS average, in that system’s own spread) and the three z-scores are averaged. A +2.0 consensus means the team averaged +2.0 standardized units (z-score units) across the three systems. Raw scales are never mixed directly.
